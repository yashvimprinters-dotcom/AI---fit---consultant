# Fashion knowledge used by v13

This catalog is a structured styling layer, not a claim that every trend is universal.
It combines established fashion color-coordination principles with current 2026 trend coverage.

Reference areas reviewed:
- Vogue: 2026 color theory and shoe trends
- Vogue India: 2026 footwear trends
- British Vogue: Spring/Summer 2026 footwear
- Who What Wear: Fall 2026 accessories
- Vogue retail-buyer trend coverage for Fall 2026
- Femina: Indianwear color-wheel coordination
- AWS Amazon Nova virtual try-on documentation for garment/accessory preservation concepts

The app should present recommendations as suggestions and keep customer choice available.
