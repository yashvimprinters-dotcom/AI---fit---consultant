# FITORA v21

Brand refresh for the AI Fit Consultant app.

## Brand
**FITORA**

**Slogan:** Your fit. Your style. Your look.

The v21 interface is based on the supplied FITORA brand reference: dark purple/pink hero, FITORA logo, simple customer-first actions, colorful primary action cards, account access, and a mobile bottom navigation.

## Logo
`static/assets/fitora-logo.png` is the supplied/reference FITORA logo treatment used in the app.

## Customer-first navigation
1. Find my size
2. Build my look
3. Try it on

Secondary features remain available through My account & more / Account:
- Sign up / profile
- Saved looks
- Feedback
- Settings
