# AI Fit Consultant v20 — Simple Customer UI

## What changed
- Main customer journey now focuses on three simple actions: Find my size, Build my look, Try it on.
- Sign up, Saved looks, Feedback, and Settings are grouped under one compact “My account & more” menu so they stay accessible without cluttering the home screen.
- Existing v18/v19 functionality and APIs are preserved.
- Mobile-first layout remains the priority.

## Notes
Profile, saved looks, feedback and settings remain browser-local in this prototype. Cloud accounts and server-side saved looks can be connected later.
